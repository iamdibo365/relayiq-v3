# Hours and locations
Lakeside Main is at 1200 Elm Street. Hours are Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. Free parking is behind the building.
Lakeside North is at 88 Oak Avenue. Hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM.
The clinic is closed on weekends and federal holidays. After hours, callers can leave a message and a nurse returns urgent calls within 2 hours.

# Telehealth
Follow-up visits can be done by video for established patients. New patient visits must be in person.
