# Insurance accepted
We are in network with Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield of Texas, and Medicare.
We verify insurance eligibility before every new appointment. If coverage cannot be confirmed before the visit, the patient may be asked to update insurance details at check-in or choose self-pay.

# Self-pay
Self-pay office visits are 150 dollars for an established patient and 225 dollars for a new patient, due at the time of service.

# Copays
Copays are collected at check-in. The copay amount depends on the patient's plan.
