# New patients
New patients should arrive 15 minutes early, bring a photo ID and insurance card, and complete the new-patient forms sent by email.

# Cancellations and no-shows
Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before the appointment. A missed appointment without 24 hours notice may incur a 25 dollar no-show fee.

# Late arrivals
Patients more than 15 minutes late may need to reschedule.
